**2. NSAIDs**
> "Classify Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drugs (NSAIDs). Write down mechanism of action, adverse effects and therapeutic uses of Aspirin." *(2021)*

**3. Pharmacokinetics**
> "What is first pass metabolism? Write in short about factors affecting the absorption of drugs." *(2022)*

**4. Anticoagulants**
> "Classify anticoagulant drugs. Write down the mechanism of action of warfarin. Mention its important therapeutic uses." *(2022)*

**5. Toxicology**
> "Write short notes on: Organophosphorous poisoning and its management." *(2022)*

**6. Adrenergic Drugs**

**7. Antimalarials**

## Notes and Limitations

- BDS pharmacology focuses heavily on Autonomic Nervous System, General Principles, NSAIDs/Analgesics, Local Anaesthetics, and Antimicrobials, which together constitute over 75% of theory marks.
