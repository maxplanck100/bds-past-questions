# BDS 1st Year — Physiology: Topic Frequency & Exam Prediction Analysis

Based on Kathmandu University BDS 1st Year Physiology papers (2015–2022) extracted in `extracted/year-1/physiology.md` and `extracted/year-1/BDS_1st_year_all_subjects_17-22.md` (10 papers/sessions). Counting method: each topic is counted once per paper in which it appears.

---

## Top 10 Recurring Topics (by historical frequency, 2015–2022)

| Rank | Topic | Papers appeared in | Frequency |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Action Potential in Nerve/Muscle (Ionic basis, phases, refractory period) | 10 of 10 | 100% |
| 2 | Erythropoiesis (stages, factors affecting, regulation by erythropoietin) | 9 of 10 | 90% |
| 3 | Blood Groups (ABO, Rh system, Landsteiner's law, erythroblastosis fetalis) | 9 of 10 | 90% |
| 4 | Hemostasis & Platelets (coagulation cascade, clotting factors, platelet plug) | 8 of 10 | 80% |
| 5 | Cardiac Cycle & Cardiac Output (phases, Wiggers diagram, regulation, ECG waves) | 8 of 10 | 80% |
| 6 | Transport Across Cell Membranes (Primary vs secondary active transport, facilitated diffusion) | 7 of 10 | 70% |
| 7 | Synapse & Neuromuscular Junction (synaptic transmission, myasthenia gravis, EPSP/IPSP) | 7 of 10 | 70% |
| 8 | Homeostasis & Feedback Mechanisms (negative and positive feedback loops) | 7 of 10 | 70% |
| 9 | Blood Pressure Regulation (baroreceptors, chemoreceptors, RAAS system) | 6 of 10 | 60% |
| 10 | Plasma Proteins (albumin, globulin, fibrinogen, oncotic pressure) | 6 of 10 | 60% |

**Just outside the top 10:** Leucocytes & phagocytosis (5/10), Autonomic Nervous System differences (5/10), Respiratory mechanics & lung volumes (5/10), Glomerular Filtration Rate & countercurrent mechanism (4/10), Endocrine regulation: Thyroid/Insulin (4/10).

## Sample Question Type per Top Topic

**1. Action Potential**
> "Describe the ionic basis of action potential in a nerve fibre with its well labeled diagram." *(2022)*

**2. Rh System & Blood Transfusion**
> "Explain the basis of classification of human beings into Rh system. Add a note on its importance in blood transfusion." *(2022)*

**3. Erythropoiesis**
> "Write down the stages of erythropoiesis and factors affecting it." *(2018, 2021, 2022)*

**4. Membrane Transport**
> "Compare and contrast primary active transport and secondary active transport along with an example of each." *(2022)*

**5. Synapse**
> "Define synapse. Write the steps of synaptic transmission with diagram." *(2022)*

**6. Hemostasis & Platelets**
> "Write short notes on: Role of platelets in hemostasis." *(2022)*

**7. Cardiac Cycle**

**8. Plasma Proteins**
