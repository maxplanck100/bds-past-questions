## Cross-Subject Recurring Themes & High-Yield Intersections

### 1. The Head, Neck & Craniofacial Axis
- **Anatomy:** Muscles of mastication (masseter, temporalis, medial/lateral pterygoid), TMJ anatomy, carotid triangle, infratemporal fossa, branchial apparatus.
- **Oral Biology:** Mandibular development around Meckel's cartilage, maxillary sinus age changes, tooth morphology (canine, 1st molars).
- **Clinical Dental Correlation:** Essential anatomical knowledge for inferior alveolar nerve block, cavity preparations, TMJ disorders, and orthodontic treatment planning.

### 2. Blood, Hemostasis & Circulation
- **Physiology:** Action potential, Rh system / Landsteiner's law, erythropoiesis, platelets in hemostasis.
- **Biochemistry:** Iron absorption, ferritin, transferrin, hepcidin regulation, heme biosynthesis.
- **Pathology:** Iron deficiency anemia lab diagnosis, septic shock, Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC).
- **Pharmacology:** Warfarin vs Heparin, Chloroquine in malaria, antiplatelet mechanisms.
- **Microbiology:** *Plasmodium* malaria thick/thin film diagnosis, *Wuchereria bancrofti* nocturnal microfilariae.

### 3. Infection Control & Inflammation
- **Microbiology:** Autoclave principle, culture media, Kirby-Bauer antibiotic sensitivity testing, oral normal flora.
- **Pathology:** Vascular and cellular events of acute inflammation, wound healing (primary vs secondary intention).
- **Pharmacology:** NSAIDs (Aspirin, selective COX-2), Local anaesthetics with adrenaline, Penicillins for odontogenic infection.

### 4. Cellular Biology, Genetics & Oncology
- **Anatomy:** Histology of spleen, lymph node, skeletal vs cardiac muscle, oogenesis.
- **Biochemistry:** Structural organization of proteins, enzyme inhibition (competitive vs non-competitive), HMP shunt.
- **Pathology:** Apoptosis (mitochondrial vs death receptor pathways), benign vs malignant tumors, AML and Hodgkin lymphoma morphology.
- **Pharmacology:** Phase I and Phase II metabolism, prodrugs, half-life.

## Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme Observations (Kathmandu University)

1. **Sectional Structure:** 
   - Most modern papers (since 2018) follow a standardized **50-mark pattern**: Section B (25 marks) and Section C (25 marks).
   - Each section typically contains:
     - One 6-mark long structured question (often split into 1+5 or 3+3 or 4+2).
     - Two 5-mark descriptive questions.
     - One short-notes question carrying 9 marks (3 notes $\times$ 3 marks each).
2. **Diagram Weightage:**
   - In Anatomy and Oral Biology, diagrams carry 2 to 3 marks out of every 5-to-6 mark question.
   - In Physiology and Biochemistry, flowcharts and biochemical reaction sequences are required for full marks.
3. **High Repetition Rate:**
   - Over 65% of theory exam questions in any given session are direct repeats or minor variations of questions asked in the previous 5 years.
