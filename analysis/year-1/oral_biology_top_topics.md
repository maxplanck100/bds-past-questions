## Sample Question Type per Top Topic

**1. Amelogenesis**
> "What is the composition of enamel? Discuss Amelogenesis in detail with diagrams." *(2022, 2023)*

**2. Tooth Morphology**
> "Describe the anatomical aspects of permanent maxillary canine with well labelled diagram." *(2024)* / "Describe in detail the morphology of permanent mandibular first molar." *(2023)*

**3. Dentinogenesis**
> "What is the composition of dentin? Describe the process of dentinogenesis in detail." *(2024)*

**4. Pulp**
> "Discuss the functions of pulp. Add a note on Plexus of Raschkow." *(2022)*

**5. Salivary Glands**
> "Classify salivary glands. Differentiate between serous and mucous acini." *(2022, 2024)*

**6. Mandible Development**
> "Discuss the derivatives of pharyngeal arch. Add a note on Meckel's cartilage." *(2024)* / "Describe the development of mandible in detail." *(2023)*

**7. Periodontal Ligament**
> "Describe the principal fiber groups of periodontal ligament with suitable diagram." *(2021, 2022)*

**8. Oral Mucosa & Gingiva**
> "Differentiate between keratinocytes and nonkeratinocytes. Describe the histology of gingiva." *(2023, 2024)*

**9. Tooth Development**
> "Enumerate the morphological stages of tooth development. Discuss about transient structures." *(2022)*

**10. Tongue Papillae**
> "Enlist the tongue papilla present in human. Add a note on any two of them with well labelled diagram." *(2023)*

## Notes and Limitations

- Oral Biology in Kathmandu University combines Dental Anatomy (Morphology of teeth, occlusal outlines, tooth numbering) and Oral Histology & Embryology (Hard tissues, soft tissues, craniofacial development).
