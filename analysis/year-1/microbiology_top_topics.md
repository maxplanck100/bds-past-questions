# BDS 1st Year — Microbiology: Topic Frequency & Exam Prediction Analysis

Based on Kathmandu University BDS 1st Year Microbiology theory papers (2015–2022) extracted in `extracted/year-1/microbiology.md` and `extracted/year-1/BDS_1st_year_all_subjects_17-22.md` (8 papers/sessions). Counting method: each topic is counted once per paper in which it appears.

---

## Top 10 Recurring Topics (by historical frequency, 2015–2022)

| Rank | Topic | Papers appeared in | Frequency |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Sterilization & Disinfection (Autoclave principle, controls, hot air oven) | 8 of 8 | 100% |
| 2 | Culture Media Classification (Enriched, selective, differential, transport) | 7 of 8 | 88% |
| 3 | Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing (Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion method) | 7 of 8 | 88% |
| 4 | Hypersensitivity Reactions (Types I–IV, anaphylaxis, Mantoux test) | 6 of 8 | 75% |
| 5 | Malaria Laboratory Diagnosis (*Plasmodium* life cycle, thick/thin smear) | 6 of 8 | 75% |
| 6 | Hepatitis Viruses (HBV structure, markers: HBsAg, HBeAg, prevention) | 5 of 8 | 63% |
| 7 | Bacterial Structure & Growth (Gram-positive vs Gram-negative wall, Growth curve) | 5 of 8 | 63% |
| 8 | Filarial Nematodes (*Wuchereria bancrofti* life cycle, nocturnal periodicity, diagnosis) | 5 of 8 | 63% |
| 9 | Fungal Infections & Dimorphism (*Candida albicans*, *Histoplasma capsulatum*) | 5 of 8 | 63% |
| 10 | Oral Microflora & Dental Caries (*Streptococcus mutans*, normal flora benefits) | 4 of 8 | 50% |

**Just outside the top 10:** Bacterial toxins: Endotoxin vs Exotoxin (4/8), Innate vs Acquired Immunity (4/8), Oncogenic Viruses (3/8), *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* diagnosis & ZN stain (3/8).

## Sample Question Type per Top Topic

**1. Sterilization & Autoclave**
> "Define sterilization and disinfection. Write down the principle and uses of autoclave." *(2022)*

**2. Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing**
> "Discuss different method of antibiotic susceptibility testing. How do you interpret Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion method?" *(2022)*

**3. Culture Media**
> "Classify different types of culture media with examples." *(2022)*

**4. Malaria Diagnosis**
> "Describe the lab diagnosis of malaria." *(2022)*

**5. Hypersensitivity**
> "Classify hypersensitivity reactions with examples. Describe Type I hypersensitivity in detail." *(2021)*

**6. Parasitology (*Wuchereria*)**
> "Describe briefly the life cycle of *Wuchereria bancrofti*. Add a note on its diagnosis." *(2022)*

**7. Bacterial Growth Curve**
> "Write short notes on: Bacterial Growth Curve." *(2022)*

**8. Mycology**

**10. Normal Flora**
> "Write short notes on: Normal flora of oral cavity and its significance." *(2022)*

## Notes and Limitations

- General Bacteriology (Sterilization, Culture media, Antibiotic susceptibility) and Parasitology/Mycology make up distinct 25-mark sections (Section B and Section C) in Kathmandu University exams.
