# BDS 1st Year — Pathology: Topic Frequency & Exam Prediction Analysis

Based on Kathmandu University BDS 1st Year Pathology theory papers (2015–2022) extracted in `extracted/year-1/pathology.md` and `extracted/year-1/BDS_1st_year_all_subjects_17-22.md` (7 papers/sessions). Counting method: each topic is counted once per paper in which it appears.

---

## Top 10 Recurring Topics (by historical frequency, 2015–2022)

| Rank | Topic | Papers appeared in | Frequency |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Neoplasia (Benign vs Malignant tumours, Routes of metastasis) | 7 of 7 | 100% |
| 2 | Acute & Chronic Inflammation (Vascular & cellular events, chemical mediators) | 7 of 7 | 100% |
| 3 | Shock (Septic shock pathogenesis, stages, morphological changes) | 6 of 7 | 86% |
| 4 | Iron Deficiency Anemia (Etiology, clinical features, detailed lab diagnosis) | 6 of 7 | 86% |
| 5 | Cell Injury & Cell Death (Necrosis types, Apoptosis mechanisms) | 6 of 7 | 86% |
| 6 | Wound Healing (Primary vs secondary intention, factors affecting healing) | 5 of 7 | 71% |
| 7 | Leukemia (Acute Myeloid Leukemia - AML vs CML lab diagnosis) | 5 of 7 | 71% |
| 8 | Lymphomas & Granulomas (Hodgkin vs Non-Hodgkin lymphoma, TB lymphadenitis) | 5 of 7 | 71% |
| 9 | Thrombosis, Embolism & Infarction (Virchow's triad, DIC etiopathogenesis) | 5 of 7 | 71% |
| 10 | Pathological Calcification (Dystrophic vs Metastatic calcification) | 4 of 7 | 57% |

**Just outside the top 10:** Atherosclerosis pathogenesis (4/7), Multiple Myeloma lab diagnosis (3/7), Amyloidosis staining and classification (3/7), Megaloblastic anemia (3/7).

## Sample Question Type per Top Topic

**1. Neoplasia**
> "Define neoplasia. Differentiate between benign and malignant tumors. Describe modes of spread of malignant tumor." *(2021, 2022)*

**2. Shock**
> "Describe the pathogenesis of septic shock, stages of shock and write down the morphological changes in shock." *(2022)*

**3. Iron Deficiency Anemia**
> "Define anemia. Write laboratory diagnosis of Iron deficiency anemia." *(2022)*

**4. Inflammation**
> "Describe vascular and cellular events of inflammation." *(2022)*

**5. Apoptosis**
> "Write mechanism of Apoptosis." *(2022)*

**6. Wound Healing**
> "Write factors affecting wound healing. Differentiate between healing by primary and secondary intention." *(2021, 2022)*

**7. Leukemia**
> "Write laboratory diagnosis on: Acute myelogenous leukemia." *(2022)*

**8. Lymphoma**
> "Write short notes on: Morphology of Hodgkin's lymphoma." *(2022)*

**9. Calcification**
> "Write short notes on: Pathological calcification." *(2022)*

**10. DIC**
> "Write short notes on: Etiopathogenesis of Disseminated intravascular coagulation." *(2022)*
